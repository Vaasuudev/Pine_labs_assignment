# REQ-101: Bulk discount

As a buyer, I want a 10% discount on a line item when I buy 10 or more units of the
same product, so that bulk purchases are rewarded.

Notes
- The discount applies per cart line, not across the whole order.
- Money stays in integer cents.
- The discounted line total is rounded half-up to the nearest whole cent (13.5 cents becomes 14).
- Use integer arithmetic only (no floats).
